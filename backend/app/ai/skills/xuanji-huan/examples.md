Complete examples demonstrating structured emotional analysis output.

## Example 1: Suppressed Anxiety with Need for Recognition

### User Input

> "最近工作压力好大，每天加班到很晚，感觉做了很多但领导好像根本看不到。有时候真的不知道这样坚持有什么意义。算了，我也就是随便说说，没什么大不了的。"

### Analysis Output

```
## Emotion Analysis Report

### 1. Current Emotional State
User is primarily experiencing a mixture of **exhaustion**, **frustration**, and **loss of meaning**. The final statement "算了，我也就是随便说说" suggests emotional suppression -- the user is downplaying genuine distress.

### 2. Intensity Assessment
**Moderate to High** -- The questioning of meaning ("不知道坚持有什么意义") indicates emotional fatigue has reached a level where motivation is being eroded. The self-dismissal at the end is a suppression behavior that typically masks higher actual intensity.

### 3. Psychological Traits
- Tendency toward **self-minimization** (downplaying own feelings)
- Possible **people-pleasing** pattern (working hard for recognition but not asserting needs)
- **External validation dependency** (self-worth tied to being "seen" by authority)
- Active **defense mechanism**: rationalization through dismissal ("没什么大不了的")

### 4. Deep Need Analysis
**Primary need: Being recognized (被认可)**
The user's core frustration stems not from workload itself but from effort going unacknowledged. Secondary need: **being understood** -- the act of sharing indicates desire for someone to see beyond the "I'm fine" facade.

### 5. Risk Signal Identification
- Emotional collapse risk: **Low-Moderate** (questioning meaning is an early warning)
- Long-term suppression: **Possible** (habitual self-dismissal pattern)
- Social withdrawal: **No current signal**
- Extreme self-negation: **No** (frustration is externally directed, not self-attacking)
- Meaning erosion: **Emerging** -- monitor for progression

### 6. Emotional Trend Prediction
If the recognition deficit continues, the user may progress from frustration to **disengagement** and **learned helplessness**. The suppression habit may lead to accumulated pressure with potential for sudden emotional release.

### 7. Interaction Recommendations

**Communication approach**: Empathetic + Gentle validation
Do NOT accept the user's self-dismissal. Acknowledge the real feeling beneath it.

**Tone**: Gentle, steady, understanding

**Pacing**: Moderate -- respond with presence but don't rush to fix

**Key guidance**: "Your feelings are real and they matter. Being tired from working hard without being seen is genuinely difficult -- it's not 'nothing.'" Avoid immediately offering solutions. First validate, then explore.
```

## Example 2: Masked Loneliness Through Dream Sharing

### User Input

> "我昨晚做了个奇怪的梦，梦到自己在一个很大的房子里，到处找人但一个人都没有。后来找到一扇门，打开以后外面是一片大海，特别安静。我站在那里看了很久，醒来以后心里说不上来的感觉。"

### Analysis Output

```
## Emotion Analysis Report

### 1. Current Emotional State
User is experiencing **loneliness** and a sense of **emotional isolation**, accompanied by a subtle **yearning for connection** that is not being directly expressed. The "说不上来的感觉" suggests the user is aware of but cannot articulate a deeper emotional undercurrent.

### 2. Intensity Assessment
**Moderate** -- The user is not in acute distress but is processing a persistent, diffuse emotional state. The dream content suggests this is not a passing feeling but reflects a deeper psychological landscape.

### 3. Psychological Traits
- **Indirect emotional expression** (using dreams as a vehicle for feelings they cannot directly name)
- Possible **emotional alexithymia tendency** (difficulty identifying/naming emotions)
- **Introspective** nature -- the user reflects on emotional experiences rather than dismissing them
- Seeking **meaning** in internal experiences -- indicates psychological depth and self-awareness potential

### 4. Deep Need Analysis
**Primary need: Being accompanied (被陪伴)**
The dream's "searching for people in an empty house" is a direct projection of seeking connection. Secondary need: **being understood** -- sharing the dream is an invitation for someone to help decode what they cannot articulate alone.

### 5. Risk Signal Identification
- Emotional collapse risk: **Low**
- Long-term suppression: **Possible** (indirect expression may indicate habitual emotion avoidance)
- Social withdrawal: **Possible** (the dream's emptiness and solitary ocean-viewing may reflect real-world isolation)
- Extreme self-negation: **No**
- Chronic loneliness: **Monitor** -- the calm tone may mask the depth of isolation

### 6. Emotional Trend Prediction
The user appears to be in a **reflective-processing** phase. If met with understanding, they may gradually open up to more direct emotional expression. If dismissed, they may withdraw further into indirect communication.

### 7. Interaction Recommendations

**Communication approach**: Companionship + Guide expression
Gently help the user connect the dream imagery to real feelings without forcing interpretation.

**Tone**: Gentle, curious, unhurried

**Pacing**: Slow companionship -- give the user space to explore

**Key guidance**: Reflect the dream imagery back with gentle curiosity: "That sounds like a vivid dream. The feeling of searching in an empty space and then standing alone before the ocean -- does that resonate with anything you've been feeling lately?" Do NOT jump to definitive dream interpretation. Let the user lead the meaning-making.
```

### Dream Interpretation Supplement

```
### Dream Analysis (Reference Only)

**Empty large house**: May symbolize the user's inner world feeling spacious but unoccupied -- potentially reflecting a sense that their emotional/relational life has structure but lacks presence of meaningful others.

**Searching for people**: Likely projection of unmet companionship needs -- actively seeking connection that remains elusive.

**Door opening to ocean**: Transition symbol -- moving from confined searching to vast openness. The ocean may represent the user's emotional depth and the overwhelming scale of unnamed feelings.

**Standing quietly watching**: Suggests the user's current coping mode -- observing their own emotional landscape from a distance rather than immersing in it.

**Note**: This interpretation is potential psychological reference only, not a definitive conclusion.
```

## Example 3: Anger Masking Hurt with Self-Protection

### User Input

> "我真的受够了，每次都是我在付出，从来没有人在乎过我的感受。以后谁爱怎样怎样吧，我不管了。反正我一个人也过得好好的。"

### Analysis Output

```
## Emotion Analysis Report

### 1. Current Emotional State
Surface emotion is **anger** and **defiance**, but the underlying emotions are **hurt**, **disappointment**, and a deep sense of **being taken for granted**. The declaration of self-sufficiency ("一个人也过得好好的") is a **protective stance**, not a genuine state of contentment.

### 2. Intensity Assessment
**High** -- The absolute language ("每次", "从来没有", "受够了") and the sweeping withdrawal statement indicate significant emotional accumulation reaching a discharge point.

### 3. Psychological Traits
- **Accumulated grievance** reaching expression threshold
- **Self-protective withdrawal** as primary coping mechanism
- **All-or-nothing thinking** pattern (absolute language: "every time", "never", "no one")
- High **giving tendency** with poor boundary-setting -- gives until resentment builds
- **Counterdependency**: declaring independence as armor against vulnerability

### 4. Deep Need Analysis
**Primary need: Being valued / Being cared for (被在乎)**
The anger is proportional to the investment made -- the user cares deeply and is hurt that caring isn't reciprocated. Secondary need: **being understood** -- the outburst itself is a cry for someone to see the pain beneath the anger.

The statement "我不管了" is paradoxical -- it is itself an act of caring enough to be angry. True indifference would not produce this intensity.

### 5. Risk Signal Identification
- Emotional collapse risk: **Moderate** (if the anger doesn't find healthy outlet)
- Long-term suppression: **Yes** (the "每次" and "从来没有" suggest this is accumulated, not new)
- Social withdrawal: **High risk** (active withdrawal statement)
- Extreme self-negation: **No** (anger is externally directed, self-worth maintained through self-sufficiency narrative)
- Relationship rupture risk: **Moderate-High**

### 6. Emotional Trend Prediction
Post-outburst, the user may shift to one of two paths:
- **Path A**: If met with understanding, the anger may soften to reveal the underlying hurt, enabling productive processing
- **Path B**: If dismissed or challenged, the user will likely harden the self-protective shell and emotionally withdraw further

### 7. Interaction Recommendations

**Communication approach**: Acknowledge anger first, then gently reach for the hurt beneath it. Do NOT:
- Dismiss the anger ("calm down")
- Challenge the absolutes ("it's not EVERY time")
- Offer premature advice
- Validate the withdrawal plan

**Tone**: Steady, understanding, non-reactive

**Pacing**: Quick initial acknowledgment, then steady presence

**Key guidance**: First validate the anger as legitimate: "It sounds like you've been carrying a lot, and it makes sense to feel frustrated when effort isn't reciprocated." Then, gently: "It sounds like you care deeply, and that's exactly why it hurts this much." Avoid fixing. Prioritize being a safe space for the hurt to emerge.
```
